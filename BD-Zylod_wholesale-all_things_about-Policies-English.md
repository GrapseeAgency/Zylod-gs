## 1. How It Works

If you've ever tried sourcing stock for your shop or online business, you know the drill — travelling to Karwan Bazar, Chalantika, or Islampur, haggling in person, carrying cash, and hoping the supplier actually has what you need in stock that day. [Your Platform Name] brings that entire wholesale market online, so sellers can list their products once and reach buyers all over the country, and buyers can source stock without leaving their shop.

Here's how it actually works, from both sides:

**For Buyers (Small Business Owners, Shop Owners, Online Resellers)**

**Step 1: Browse or Search**
Explore products by category — clothing, footwear, cosmetics, electronics accessories, home goods, and more — or search directly for what you need. Every listing shows the seller's price tiers, minimum order quantity (MOQ), and stock availability upfront.

**Step 2: Compare Sellers**
For most products, you'll find multiple sellers offering similar items at different price points. Compare based on price, MOQ, seller rating, and location, so you can choose what works best for your budget and business.

**Step 3: Place Your Order**
Add products to your cart, confirm quantities, and check out. You'll see your total cost clearly — product price, any applicable delivery charge, and platform fee — before you confirm.

**Step 4: Pay Securely**
Pay through bKash, Nagad, bank transfer, or card. Your payment is held securely by the platform until your order is confirmed as received, so you're protected if something goes wrong.

**Step 5: Receive Your Stock**
Sellers ship directly to you through our courier partners, or you can arrange pickup if the seller is local to you. Track your order status right from your dashboard.

**Step 6: Confirm and Review**
Once you've received and checked your order, confirm receipt so the seller gets paid, and leave a review to help other buyers.

**For Sellers (Manufacturers, Wholesalers, Distributors)**

**Step 1: Register Your Business**
Create a seller account with your trade licence, NID, and business details. We verify sellers to keep the marketplace trustworthy for buyers.

**Step 2: List Your Products**
Upload photos, set your price tiers based on quantity, define your MOQ, and specify your delivery areas and lead times.

**Step 3: Receive and Fulfil Orders**
When a buyer orders, you'll get a notification. Confirm the order, pack it, and hand it to our courier partner or your own delivery team.

**Step 4: Get Paid**
Once the buyer confirms receipt, payment is released to your account, usually within [X] business days.

Throughout the process, our support team is available to both buyers and sellers if anything needs sorting out.

## 5. Bulk Pricing Guide

One of the biggest advantages of buying wholesale through [Your Platform Name] is transparent, tiered pricing — here's exactly how it works.

**1. Tiered Pricing by Quantity**
Sellers on our platform set price tiers based on order quantity — the more you order, the lower your per-unit cost. For example, a seller might price a product at ৳120/piece for 20–49 units, ৳100/piece for 50–199 units, and ৳85/piece for 200+ units. These tiers are shown clearly on each product listing.

**2. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)**
Every product has an MOQ set by the seller — this is the smallest amount you can order. MOQs vary by seller and product category, and are shown upfront on the listing so there's no confusion at checkout.

**3. What Determines Your Final Price**
- **Base product price** (based on your quantity tier)
- **Platform fee**, where applicable (some categories may include a small buyer-side service charge, clearly shown before payment)
- **Delivery charge**, based on distance, weight, and delivery speed selected
- **VAT**, where applicable under Bangladeshi tax law for the product category

The full cost breakdown is shown before you confirm your order — no surprise charges added afterwards.

**4. Delivery Speed Options**
- **Standard delivery** — typically [3–7] days, most economical
- **Express delivery** — typically [1–3] days, higher delivery charge, useful when you're low on stock and need it fast

**5. Comparing Sellers**
Since multiple sellers often list similar products, it's worth comparing price tiers, MOQ, seller ratings, and delivery location before ordering. A seller closer to your location may offer faster, cheaper delivery even if their per-unit price is slightly higher.

**6. Combining Orders**
If you're ordering from the same seller across multiple products, combining them into a single order can reduce your overall delivery cost, since it's often charged per shipment rather than per item.

**7. Tips to Get Better Pricing**
- Order at or just above a price-tier threshold to unlock the next discount level.
- Build a relationship with reliable sellers for better service on repeat orders.
- Watch for seasonal or festival promotions that many sellers run around Eid and other peak shopping periods.
- If you're a high-volume buyer, message the seller directly through the platform to ask about custom pricing for large recurring orders.

## 6. Seller Guide

Whether you're a garment manufacturer in Narayanganj, a cosmetics distributor in Dhaka, or a small factory owner anywhere in Bangladesh, this guide will help you get the most out of selling on [Your Platform Name].

**1. Setting Up Your Seller Account**
Register with your trade licence, NID, business address, and a bank or mobile financial account for receiving payments. Verified sellers get a trust badge that helps buyers choose your listings with confidence.

**2. Creating Strong Product Listings**
- Use clear, well-lit photos — actual product photos perform far better than manufacturer stock images.
- Write accurate descriptions, including material, size options, colour variants, and packaging details.
- Set realistic price tiers based on your actual production or sourcing cost plus margin — undercutting too aggressively can hurt your business long-term.
- Set an MOQ that makes sense for your production or stocking capacity, not just the lowest number possible.
- Keep your stock quantity updated so buyers don't order what you can't fulfil.

**3. Pricing Strategy**
Buyers actively compare listings, so competitive, clearly tiered pricing helps you get chosen. Consider:
- Offering a slightly better rate at higher quantity tiers to encourage bigger orders
- Reviewing your pricing periodically against similar listings on the platform
- Factoring in your delivery costs and lead time when setting your final price

**4. Managing Orders**
- Confirm new orders promptly — buyers can see how quickly you typically respond, and this affects your seller rating.
- Pack goods securely to prevent damage during transit, especially for fragile or bulk-packed items.
- Hand off to your chosen courier partner or arrange your own delivery within the lead time you've committed to.
- Keep your order status updated on the platform so buyers can track progress.

**5. Getting Paid**
Once a buyer confirms receipt (or the confirmation window passes without a dispute), your payment is released to your registered account, typically within [X] business days. You can track your pending and completed payments from your seller dashboard.

**6. Handling Disputes**
If a buyer raises an issue about your order, respond promptly and in good faith — most disputes are resolved quickly when sellers engage constructively. Repeated unresolved disputes can affect your seller rating and, in serious cases, your account status.

**7. Building Your Reputation**
- Consistent on-time fulfilment and accurate listings build a strong seller rating over time.
- Encourage buyers to leave reviews after successful orders — a strong review history is one of the biggest factors buyers consider.
- Respond to buyer messages promptly, even before an order is placed; many buyers message sellers with questions before committing to a large order.

**8. Growing Your Sales**
- Keep your catalogue updated with new products and seasonal items.
- Watch for platform promotions or featured-seller opportunities you can apply for.
- As your order volume grows, our team can advise you on scaling your listings, logistics, and pricing structure.

**9. Need Help?**
Our seller support team is available via [phone/WhatsApp/live chat] during [business hours] to help with listings, disputes, payments, or anything else you need.
